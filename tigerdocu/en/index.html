Privacy Policy

TigerDocu (com.handfirst.tigerdocu) · Effective November 1, 2026 · Developer: FutureSoftLab

TigerDocu has no accounts and does not collect your name, contact details, precise location or any other information that directly identifies you. Google AdMob uses your advertising ID to show ads, Google Analytics collects anonymous usage statistics so we can improve the game, and our own server stores a random ID created on your device together with your puzzle progress to show your worldwide rank.

1. Information we collect

TigerDocu itself does not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or call history.

To show ads in the app, Google AdMob may process:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information and approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Ad impressions and clicks

To improve the game, Google Analytics for Firebase processes anonymous usage statistics such as:

  • Basic usage — installs, app launches, time spent in the app
  • Puzzle progress — stage number, board size, difficulty, time taken, completed or abandoned
  • Feature usage — hints used, settings changed (theme, colour-blind assist, etc.)
  • General device information — device model, OS version, country
  • App instance ID and advertising ID

These statistics contain no name, email or similar personal details, and we only look at them in aggregate (for example, which stage players find too hard). This information is collected and processed by Google directly; we do not view it in a form that identifies individual users.

Finally, for the worldwide rank feature, a server operated by the developer (game.fastmax.work) stores:

  • A random identifier (a 32-character random string) created on your device the first time the app runs
  • The highest stage you have reached and your total time for stages 1–10 (in seconds)

This identifier is randomly generated. It is not a hardware ID or your advertising ID. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, and a new one is created if you install it again.

2. Accounts and sign-in

There is no sign-up or sign-in. The leaderboard has no display names, so no other player's name or record ever appears on screen. You only see your own rank and the total number of players.

3. Network use

Every puzzle is generated on your device, and the game works fully without an internet connection. The network is used only to load ads, send usage statistics and exchange rank information. When you are offline, ads and ranks simply do not appear; the game itself keeps working.

4. Information stored on your device

  • The state of the puzzle in progress
  • Per-stage records — stages cleared, time, hearts left, hints used, stars, attempts
  • Settings such as light/dark theme

Of these, only your highest stage and your stages 1–10 total time are sent to the server for ranking (see section 7). Everything else stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

5. Advertising

The app shows a Google AdMob banner at the bottom of the screen. When you run out of an item (undo, hint, etc.), you may choose to watch a rewarded ad to refill it; rewarded ads are only shown when you tap to watch one. AdMob uses your advertising ID to serve relevant ads.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

To opt out of personalised ads, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or turn off ads personalisation) on your device.

6. Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which puzzles are too hard and where players stop playing, so that we can tune difficulty and screens. The items collected are listed in section 1. We do not send values that identify you, and because there is no sign-in we cannot look up any particular user.

You can limit this collection by deleting your advertising ID in your device settings.

7. Worldwide rank

Your highest stage and your stages 1–10 total time are uploaded to the developer’s server (game.fastmax.work) to show where you stand among all players.

There are no display names. Other players’ names or records are never shown, and there is no way for users to post or upload content.

The server does not log IP addresses (they may appear temporarily in error logs if a server error occurs). Other players’ identifiers are never included in any response.

To have your record deleted, contact us at the address below. Once you uninstall the app the identifier on your device is gone, and the record can no longer be linked to you.

8. Where data is stored

The rank server is located in the Republic of Korea. If you use the app from another country, the random identifier and records described in section 1 are transferred to and stored in Korea. Data processed by Google is handled according to Google’s own policies and may be stored in Google data centres worldwide.

9. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. Google (AdMob and Analytics) processes the information in section 1 only as needed to show ads and analyse usage. Rank records are kept only on the server the developer operates and are not passed to anyone else.

10. Children

This app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not collect names, contact details or other identifying information from users of any age, and we do not build profiles or target ads at children.

11. Permissions

The app does not request sensitive runtime permissions such as camera, precise location, storage, contacts or microphone. It declares only internet access and use of the advertising ID, for ads, usage statistics and ranks.

12. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings to stop personalised ads and limit analytics.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your device and the random identifier.
  • Email us to ask for your rank record to be deleted.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version and its effective date on this page.
